Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Shvarzman Consulting do?

Shvarzman Consulting is an independent contact center, AI and Salesforce consultancy based in Miami, FL. It helps enterprises move customer service from on-premise systems to Salesforce Service Cloud, Service Cloud Voice, Agentforce AI and modern CCaaS platforms, covering strategy, architecture and hands-on build under one consultant.

What is the difference between an advisory and an implementation engagement?

Advisory covers vendor and platform selection, architecture, roadmaps and the business case that wins executive buy-in. Implementation is the hands-on build of Service Cloud, Service Cloud Voice and Agentforce agents across voice and messaging channels, plus CCaaS integrations. End-to-end transformation combines both, from discovery through go-live and beyond.

Which platforms do you work with?

Salesforce Service Cloud, Service Cloud Voice, Agentforce Contact Center and Agentforce, including Agentforce Voice agents on the phone channel. Partner telephony and CCaaS platforms: Amazon Connect, Genesys, Five9, NICE CXone, Talkdesk, Cisco and RingCentral, with AI from Agentforce and OpenAI. Channels include voice, chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Apple Messages for Business.

What results can a contact center expect from AI deflection?

Typical results across my programs, comparing on-premise operations before transformation with cloud plus AI after: customer satisfaction from about 65% to 85% or more, average handle time from about 9.2 to 5.5 minutes, and first-contact resolution from about 58% to 75% or more. Every engagement defines its own KPI targets up front and tracks them after go-live.

Do you work with companies outside Florida?

Yes. Shvarzman Consulting is based in Miami, FL and works with enterprises across the United States, remotely and on site. Past programs include Southwest, McKesson, Norwegian Cruise Line, Princess Cruises, Patagonia, UCSF, Amica, Avetta, Texas Oncology and Mom's Meals.
