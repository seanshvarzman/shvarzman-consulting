What Agentforce is

Agentforce is Salesforce's platform for building and running AI agents that take actions, not just answer questions. A service agent is configured with topics it may handle, instructions for each, and actions it can call, such as Flows, Apex and prompt templates. It is grounded in CRM records and knowledge through Data Cloud, runs behind the Einstein Trust Layer, and hands conversations to a human in Service Cloud with the full transcript when it should.

Two sides matter for a contact center. Customer-facing agents work on web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app messaging and voice. Rep-facing assistance drafts replies, summarizes cases and surfaces knowledge while a human handles the conversation. The second is lower risk and often the better first step.

Where it pays back

  • High volumes of a small number of intents: order status, appointment changes, password and access issues, billing questions, document requests.
  • Knowledge that already exists but agents spend minutes finding.
  • After-hours volume that today waits for the morning queue.
  • Agent after-call work that is mostly summarizing what was just said.

Where it does not pay back: intents that need judgment or empathy first, processes that are not defined well enough for a human to follow consistently, and knowledge bases that are out of date. Fixing those is part of the work, not a reason to skip it.

Mistakes I see in Agentforce programs

  • Starting with every intent. The first release should cover a handful of high-volume, low-risk topics and prove resolution before widening.
  • Grounding on a stale knowledge base. The agent is only as right as the articles behind it. Knowledge hygiene is phase one.
  • Measuring containment instead of resolution. A customer who gives up is "contained". Resolution and post-conversation CSAT are the numbers that matter.
  • Hiding the human. A clear, fast path to a person with the transcript attached is what makes customers trust the agent at all.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Agentforce and Einstein Bots?

Einstein Bots follow dialog trees you script in advance. Agentforce agents reason over instructions and knowledge to decide what to do, call actions in Salesforce, and handle phrasing they were never scripted for. Agentforce replaces the bot for most new service deployments.

What deflection rate should we expect?

It depends on your intent mix and knowledge quality. I plan around roughly 30% of routine contacts as a conservative starting point and set the real target per topic during discovery, then measure resolution rather than containment.

Does Agentforce need Data Cloud?

Grounding in CRM records and knowledge articles can start without a separate data program, but Data Cloud is how you bring in data from outside Salesforce and unify identities. I scope it based on where the answers actually live.

How do you keep the agent from making things up?

Tight topic scoping, instructions that require grounding before answering, the Einstein Trust Layer, guardrails on out-of-scope requests, and a human handoff that is easy to reach. Then test with real transcripts before launch.

Can Agentforce work on voice?

Yes, on Service Cloud Voice. I recommend proving topics on chat and messaging first, then extending the same agent to voice.
